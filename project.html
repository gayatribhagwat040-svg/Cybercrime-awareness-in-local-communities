⚠️ Common Cyber Crimes

Understand common online threats and how scammers target people.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to steal your personal information.

📱

OTP Fraud

Never share your OTP, PIN or banking password with anyone.

💸

UPI Fraud

Fraudsters may trick you into approving a payment or sharing sensitive details.

👤

Digital Arrest Scam

Criminals pretend to be police or officials and demand money through calls.

🚨 Important: Digital Arrest Scam Warning

Police or government officials do not conduct arrests through video calls or demand money to cancel an arrest. If someone threatens you with a digital arrest, do not share money, OTPs or personal details. Report suspicious financial cyber fraud immediately by calling 1930.

🔐 Stay Safe Online

Follow these simple habits to protect yourself.

🔑

Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

📵

Never Share OTP

Banks and genuine services will never need your OTP over a call.

🔗

Check Links

Avoid clicking suspicious links received through messages.

📲

Update Devices

Keep your phone, laptop and applications updated.
